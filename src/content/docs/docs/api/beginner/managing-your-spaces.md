---
title: Managing Your Spaces
description: Switch, make, rename, recolor and delete Spaces on your computer and your phone.
---

A **Space** keeps one part of your life apart from the rest: its own tabs, bookmarks, pinned pages, drives and Persona. You always have a **Personal** Space, and you can make as many others as you like (Work, Home, a side project). Your Spaces are the same on all your [linked Devices](/docs/api/advanced/devices-and-the-vault/).

## On your computer

Click the **Spaces** button at the top of the tab bar (the colored dot with the Space's name) to open the Spaces menu.

![The Spaces menu, with the pen button on the Work Space](/guides/spaces/desktop-list.webp)

- **Switch:** click a Space.
- **Make one:** choose **New space**, type a name, pick a color, then **Create**.
- **Rename or recolor:** point at a Space and click its pen button. Change the name or the color, then **Save**.

![Editing a Space: its name, its color, and Delete space](/guides/spaces/desktop-edit.webp)

## On your phone

Tap the Space's name in the address bar to open the Space switcher.

![The phone's Space switcher, with a pen button on each Space](/guides/spaces/phone-list.webp)

- **Switch:** tap a Space.
- **Make one:** tap **New space**, type a name, pick a color, then **Create**.
- **Rename or recolor:** tap a Space's pen button, change the name or the color, then **Save**.

![Editing a Space on the phone](/guides/spaces/phone-edit.webp)

## Delete a Space

Open the Space's edit form (the pen button), choose **Delete space**, then confirm.

![Nomad asks before it deletes a Space](/guides/spaces/desktop-delete-confirm.webp)

Deleting a Space closes its tabs and takes it off all your Devices. Its bookmarks, pinned pages and drive list go with it, and it can't be undone. If you were in it, Nomad moves you to your Personal Space.

Your **Personal** Space can be renamed, but not deleted.

## Leave a shared Space

A Space you [share with other people](/docs/api/advanced/sharing-a-space/) isn't yours to delete: the others keep it. Its edit form has **Leave space** instead. Leaving takes it off all your Devices. To come back, you need a new invite. If you're its only owner, make someone else an owner first.

## Renaming a shared Space

A new name or color for a shared Space is yours: it shows on your Devices. The other people in the Space keep their own.
