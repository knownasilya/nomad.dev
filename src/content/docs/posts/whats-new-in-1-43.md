Everything below is in [Nomad 1.43](https://github.com/knownasilya/nomad/releases/tag/v1.43.0), on desktop and Android. Your app updates itself, or you can download it from the release page.

## Set up a home

My Library → Sharing has a new **Set up a home** card. Give the home a name and your own name, press **Set up**, and Nomad makes a shared Space for the people you live with. It comes with a **Groceries** list and a **House info** note for the Wi‑Fi, the bins and emergency contacts, both pinned. Then it takes you straight to inviting the others.

![The Sharing page in My Library, with the Set up a home card above Join a shared Space](/posts/whats-new-in-1-43/set-up-home.webp)

## Task lists in Notes

Ticking an item in a note's task list moves it below the items that are still open, and unticking it moves it back up. **Clear done** removes every ticked item at once. In a shared Space, each ticked item shows who ticked it, so you know who already bought the coffee. This works on your computer and on your phone.

![The Groceries note, with four open items and two ticked ones marked Sam and Ilya, and a Clear done (2) button](/posts/whats-new-in-1-43/task-list.webp)

## Jobs that tell everyone

A Job in a shared Space can now notify everyone in it, not just you. Tick **Everyone in Home** under Notify, and each person's phone shows what the Job sends, like "bins go out tonight". Keep private notes out of these Jobs, because everyone sees whatever they say.

![A Job called Bins night, run Tuesdays at 6 PM, with Everyone in Home ticked under Notify](/posts/whats-new-in-1-43/job-everyone.webp)

## Everything else

- When you edit a note live on your phone, the computer now always shows the phone's cursor and selection.
- Settings shows the Auto Updater heading once instead of twice.

The full list of changes is in the [changelog](https://github.com/knownasilya/nomad/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md). If something doesn't work the way you expect, [open an issue](https://github.com/knownasilya/nomad/issues).
