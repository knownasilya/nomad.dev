Everything below is in [Nomad 1.42](https://github.com/knownasilya/nomad/releases/tag/v1.42.0), on desktop and Android. Your app updates itself, or you can download it from the release page.

## Live notes on your phone

[Nomad 1.41](/posts/whats-new-in-1-41/) made an open note live on your computers. Now your phone joins in too. Open a note on the phone while it's open on your computer, and you see the computer's edits as they're typed, with a cursor labelled with the computer's name. What you type on the phone shows up on the computer the same way.

![A note on the phone, with a MacBook Air cursor on one line and the AI's violet cursor adding a line, and "AI is editing…" at the top](/posts/whats-new-in-1-42/phone-live-note.webp)

When your AI is changing the note, you see its violet **AI** cursor on the phone as well, and the top of the note says **AI is editing…**. The round badges next to it show who else has the note open: the AI and each of your computers.

## Everything else

On `nomad://` pages, the address bar now shows the Nomad mark in a quiet gray instead of an old blue ring.

![The address bar on Nomad Library, Settings and the start page, with the gray Nomad mark](/posts/whats-new-in-1-42/url-bar-mark.webp)

The full list of changes is in the [changelog](https://github.com/knownasilya/nomad/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md). If something doesn't work the way you expect, [open an issue](https://github.com/knownasilya/nomad/issues).
