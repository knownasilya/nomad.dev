Everything below is in [Nomad 1.35](https://github.com/knownasilya/nomad/releases/tag/v1.35.0), on desktop and Android. Your app updates itself, or you can download it from the release page.

## Make a Persona on your phone

[Personas](/posts/whats-new-in-1-34/) arrived in 1.34, but you could only make one on your computer. Now the phone's Personas screen has a **+** in its header and a **New Persona** row. Give the Persona a name, a picture, a few words, a label only you see, and an email if you want sites to be able to ask for it.

![The phone's New Persona form, with a picture, name, label, about and email](/posts/whats-new-in-1-35/phone-new-persona.webp)

The phone makes the Persona's profile drive itself, so you can log in with it right away. Keep the phone online for a little while afterward so your computer can fetch the new drive. Your first Persona becomes your default.

## Edit a Persona on any device

A Persona's name, picture and words used to change only on the device that made it. Now every one of your devices can write every Persona's profile drive, so you can edit one wherever you are. On the phone, tap a Persona for **Edit**, **Make default** and **Delete**.

![The phone's Personas screen with a Persona's menu open, and the Edit Persona form](/posts/whats-new-in-1-35/phone-edit-persona.webp)

A device that can't write a Persona's drive yet asks your other devices, and the first one online adds it. Until then, the phone shows that it's waiting. You can still change the label and email in the meantime. When a device leaves your Vault, it loses access to your Personas too.
